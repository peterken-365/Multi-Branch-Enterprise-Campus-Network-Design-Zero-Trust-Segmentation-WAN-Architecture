Lab console — replay of the recorded CLI sessions

Pick a device, type a command, and see what the six golden configs produce. Try the packet policy trace on the right to follow any flow through the zero-trust gateways.

What this is (and is not). This console replays a recorded reference session per device — it is not a live device and does not execute Cisco IOS. Commands outside the recorded set answer honestly (invalid input, or a pointer to the recorded alternative). To run anything live, build the lab from the build guide — the golden configs in 02-switching-and-routing-design/configs/ are the source of every transcript here.
