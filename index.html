01 · The business problem

From a flat office LAN to an engineered campus network

Savannah Logistics outgrew its single flat office router. The legacy network concentrated every employee, guest phone and server into one broadcast domain — with no redundancy, no encrypted management, no central time or logging, and no structured IP plan.

Legacy risk 01

One flat broadcast domain

Guest phones, finance workstations and payroll servers all shared VLAN 1 — one infected device could scan the entire business. Fix: seven VLANs with a zero-trust extended ACL policy enforced at every gateway.

Legacy risk 02

No loop-free redundancy

Default STP with no root planning — a single link event could silence the office. Fix: Rapid PVST+ with the multilayer core explicitly pinned as Root Bridge, PortFast and BPDU Guard on every edge port.

Legacy risk 03

Unprotected management plane

Telnet and HTTP management with no central time or logging — sessions unencrypted, logs untrustworthy. Fix: SSHv2-only VTY bound to the IT VLAN, with NTP + Syslog centralized at 10.20.30.11.

Legacy risk 04

No addressing discipline

Oversized subnets, wasted space, no growth path and no cloud headroom. Fix: a full VLSM architecture from 10.20.0.0/16 sized to real demand, with reserved blocks for the Azure S2S VPN extension.

03 · Zero-trust traffic policy

Nothing moves unless a gateway permits it
