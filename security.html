Security policy — gateway ACLs, L2 hardening, management plane

Deny-by-default at every enforcement point, with denies logged to the central Syslog so the hit counters double as an audit trail. Source of truth: acl-traffic-matrix.md · layer2-security-policy.md.

DHCP Snooping runs on VLANs 10/20/40 (SW1), 30 (SW2), 50 (SW3) with no ip dhcp snooping information option (Packet Tracer relay compatibility). DAI validates ARP on untrusted ports against the snooping bindings — and against static ip source binding entries on SW2, because servers are statically addressed and never speak DHCP.

Management plane

Two independent locks protect every device:

  1. Gateway lock — MGMT-PLANE on MLS Vlan99 (out) and the isolation denies upstream: only 10.20.10.0/26 can even reach the management VLAN, and only for SSH/22 + ICMP. NTP replies from 10.20.30.11 are the sole other permitted flow (the switches are NTP clients).
  2. Door lock — MGMT-ACCESS via access-class on every VTY: the same /26, with deny any log. Even a packet that somehow bypassed the gateways cannot open a session from anywhere else.

Telnet is not enabled anywhere (transport input ssh only); HTTP/HTTPS device management is off; a legal MOTD banner warns on every session; login block-for 120 attempts 3 within 60 throttles brute force.
