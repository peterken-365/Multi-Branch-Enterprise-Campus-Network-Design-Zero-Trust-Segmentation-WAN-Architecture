VLSM IP Addressing Architecture

Every segment is carved from the private block 10.20.0.0/16 by real demand — sized, documented, and reserved for structured growth. Convention: each VLAN owns the 10.20.<VLAN-ID>.0 block and carves the required prefix out of it.

Why VLAN 99 is split per site: a Layer-2 VLAN cannot span the routed WAN. Growth headroom (deliberate, structured)

  1. VLAN 20 (Finance): currently /25 (126 hosts). The full 10.20.20.0/24 block is unused outside this /25, so growth = re-mask the SVI + widen the DHCP scope. No renumbering.
  2. VLAN 10 (IT): /26 today, /25 or /24 available in the same block.
  3. VLAN 40 (Guest): the dynamic pool starts at .100 so the lower half absorbs admin/appliance statics without disturbing leases.
  4. Branch: VLAN 50 can grow to /24 within 10.20.50.0/24.
  5. Site replication: the 10.20.<VLAN>.0 convention leaves room for a future second branch (e.g., Kisumu) to reuse VLAN IDs with new site octets instead of redesigning the plan.
  6. Cloud: 10.20.200.0/24 and 10.20.254.8/30 are ring-fenced now so the Azure S2S VPN needs zero re-addressing later.
